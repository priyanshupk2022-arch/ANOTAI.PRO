# 🤝 MERCHANT ONBOARDING GUIDE

Welcome to ANOTAI! Follow these 5 steps to get your AI agents up and running.

## 1. Playbook Setup
Go to **Playbook** in the app menu. This is where you tell the AI who you are.
- **Brand Voice**: Is your brand witty, professional, or minimalist?
- **Niche**: Tell the AI you sell "High-end skincare" or "Sustainable fashion".

## 2. COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)
AI cannot protect your profits if it doesn't know your costs.
- Go to **Inventory > COGS**.
- Sync your products from Shopify.
- Enter the cost for your top 20 best-sellers first.
- *Pro Tip*: You can upload a CSV for bulk updates.

## 3. Enable the Widget
To allow the **Personal Shopper** to chat with customers:
- Go to your Shopify Admin > Online Store > Themes.
- Click **Customize** on your current theme.
- Go to **App Embeds** on the left sidebar.
- Toggle **ANOTAI Widget** to ON and save.

## 4. Setup Cart Recovery
- Go to **Agents > Cart Sniper**.
- Enable the recovery sequence.
- Set your **Max Discount** (e.g., 15%).

## 5. Review the Action Queue
Wait for your first customer interaction. Check the **Action Queue** to see what the AI recommends!
